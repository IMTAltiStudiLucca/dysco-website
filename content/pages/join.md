## PhD

DYSCO is in charge of the [Learning and Control](https://sys.imtlucca.it/program-overview/learning-and-control-lc) track of the PhD program in Systems Science at IMT Lucca. The track covers machine learning and the control of dynamical systems based on numerical optimization.

PhD positions are advertised in the yearly call of the School. See the [call for applications and deadlines](https://sys.imtlucca.it/how-to-apply_1).

## Postdocs and visitors

For postdoctoral positions or visits, write to the faculty member closest to your interests (see the [People](#people) page) or to [dysco@imtlucca.it](mailto:dysco@imtlucca.it).
